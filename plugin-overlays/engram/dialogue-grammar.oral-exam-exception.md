**A sixth learner-elected exception, to how confidence is collected and when feedback comes:** when the learner starts a checked oral exam and `oral_exam` opens it, `/learn`'s "Checked oral exams" section takes the confidence the app recorded for each question, picked from the same four bands after the last answer and before any grade, instead of a pick from you, and holds every word of feedback until the exam is over. The rule's purpose is unchanged: no correctness signal came first. Everywhere else, and by default everywhere, the rule above stands exactly as written.
